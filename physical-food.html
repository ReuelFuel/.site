Magnesium: the mineral modern diets are missing

Magnesium is a cofactor in over 300 enzymatic reactions — yet an estimated 80% of adults don't get enough. This post traces what happens when cellular magnesium falls below threshold: impaired ATP synthesis, dysregulated calcium signalling, and a metabolic environment increasingly hospitable to disease.
